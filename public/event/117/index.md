# Introduction to Magic


**When:** May 9–Jun 27
**Venue:** [Chicago Magic Lounge](https://www.chicagomagiclounge.com/)
**Address:** 5050 North Clark Street, Chicago, IL 60640
**Price:** $350 per person + $4.99 processing fee
**Performers:** Abby Segal (host), Kevin Kapinos (host)
**Tags:** class-or-workshop, 21-plus, theater

A six-week beginner magic course covering foundational techniques, creative thinking, and self-expression. The course includes all materials and concludes with a student showcase on June 27 where participants can perform for friends and family.
