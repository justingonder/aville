# Midsummer Flower Crown Workshop


**When:** Sunday, May 31 · 3pm
**Venue:** [Swedish American Museum](https://www.swedishamericanmuseum.org/)
**Address:** 5211 N Clark St, Chicago, IL 60640
**Tags:** one-off, class-or-workshop, family-friendly, daytime, cultural

A workshop to make flower crowns in preparation for Midsommarfest. All materials are included. The workshop celebrates Midsummer (Midsommar), the Swedish celebration of the summer season.
