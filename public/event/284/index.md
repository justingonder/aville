# Scandinavian Jam


**When:** 4th Sunday of the month · 1pm
**Venue:** [Swedish American Museum](https://www.swedishamericanmuseum.org/)
**Address:** 5211 N Clark St, Chicago, IL 60640
**Tags:** monthly-recurring, live-music, daytime, family-friendly, cultural

A monthly afternoon of traditional Scandinavian roots music at the Swedish American Museum. Instruction runs from 1 p.m. to 1:45 p.m., followed by open jam session. Musicians and instructors host the event.
