Contemporary variable grotesque

Bricolage Grotesque is very much of this moment — used by independent brands, studios, and editorial projects that want presence without pretension. Its slightly wide proportions give it warmth. Optical sizing means it works from tiny labels to big headers.

Precise, confident grotesque

Instrument Sans is Google's newest grotesque — clean and structured without feeling cold. It reads very well at small sizes (great for card metadata) and has quiet authority at display size. Most neutral of the three: lets the content lead, the type stays out of the way.
